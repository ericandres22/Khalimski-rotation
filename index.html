Empty cells: cells whose centre lies in the continuously rotated region but that receive no source cell. Interior leaves out a band two upright steps wide along the region’s edge, where cells naturally enter and leave the region.

Radial error: R − R′, with R the distance from a source centre to the pivot and R′ the distance from its digital image to the pivot. Positive means the cell moved inwards.
